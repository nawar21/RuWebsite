The problem

Every factory tells the same robot story.

01

They bought one. It does one job.

Years later it is still doing that one job, because changing it means an integrator, months, and a new bill.

02

Changing the job costs more than the robot did.

A $35k arm becomes a $60k+ project once it is integrated — and the bill restarts every time the product changes.

03

Nobody wants to hire for the repetitive work.

The hardware is on the floor. The people to run the dull stations are not. The intelligence is what is missing.

The solution

We don’t sell you a robot. We make the one you have smart.

A physical AI brain that runs on the arm you already paid for. Diagnose the mission, prove it in simulation, run it on your line, learn from every mistake.

How it works

Diagnose. Then run it.

  1. 1

    Diagnose

    We walk your floor with you, pick the mission that hurts most, and agree the KPIs before anything moves.

  2. 2

    Simulate

    Our cameras map your line and parts. The mission is planned and proven in simulation first — no production downtime.

  3. 3

    Run

    Your robot does the job on the real line. Live in days, not months.

  4. 4

    Learn

    When something goes wrong, it remembers. It won’t do it twice. The robot gets more reliable every shift.

Every mission learned in one factory makes the next one faster.
